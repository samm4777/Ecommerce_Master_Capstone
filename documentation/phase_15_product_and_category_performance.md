# Phase 15: Product & Category Performance Analysis


## Objective

The purpose of this phase is to evaluate product and category performance from multiple business perspectives.

The analysis does not rely only on revenue. Categories are evaluated using:

- Revenue contribution
- Sales volume
- Customer satisfaction
- Delivery performance
- Freight burden


---

# Analysis Areas


## 1. Category Revenue Performance

Metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Units Sold


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories generating the highest commercial value.


---

## 2. Category Volume Performance

Metrics:

- Units Sold
- Order Count


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories with strong customer demand regardless of revenue size.


---

## 3. Customer Satisfaction Analysis

Metrics:

- Average Review Rating
- Number of Reviews


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories with strong customer satisfaction.

High sales categories are not automatically considered successful if customer ratings are poor.


---

## 4. Poorly Rated Categories

Method:

Categories with sufficient review volume are analyzed using average review score.


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories requiring investigation due to lower customer satisfaction.


---

## 5. Delivery Performance by Category

Metrics:

- Average Delivery Days


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories where customers experience longer delivery times.


---

## 6. Freight Burden Analysis

Metrics:

- Total Freight Cost
- Average Freight Cost
- Freight Percentage of Merchandise Value


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories where logistics costs represent a significant portion of product value.


---

## 7. Low Performing Categories

Metrics:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Ratings


Business Purpose:

Identifies categories requiring additional business review.


---

# Data Sources Used

SQL Tables:

- stg.products
- stg.order_items
- stg.orders
- stg.reviews


---

# Conclusion

The category performance analysis provides a balanced view of product performance.

Categories are evaluated across financial contribution, customer demand, customer experience, operational performance, and logistics impact.
